I am a Postdoctoral Associate at the ILR School at Cornell University. I received my PhD in Economics from Cornell University in 2026, and my BSc and MSc in Economics from Bocconi University.

My research interests are in **applied microeconomics**, **public economics**, **political economy**, and **industrial organization**. I use tools from mechanism design and information economics to study how institutions are designed and how they shape behavior. In my [job market paper](/files/Billari_Dont_Try_Again.pdf), I study limits on the number of attempts at licensing and civil-service exams, such as the U.S. bar exam, as a way to screen candidates' skills.

You can find my papers on the [Research](/research/) page and my [CV here](/files/Francesco_Billari_CV.pdf).
